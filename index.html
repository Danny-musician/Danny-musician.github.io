About

Music has been a long-running part of my life: discipline, stage presence, ensemble listening, and expressive control.

With over a decade of experience, I am a classically trained pianist and choir singer, as well as the former choir conductor for AiYue Chorus at BUPT. My musical journey also includes performing as a jazz and R&B vocalist with the NUS Jazz Band.

My musical journey began when I started learning classical piano at the age of four. I studied under Mrs. Lü Zhen (吕真), Associate Professor of Piano at the School of Music, South China Normal University, and Visiting Professor at the Central Conservatory of Music.

Later, I explored popular music through pop piano arrangements and Chinese folk singing with Mrs. Zhang Xiuli (张秀丽), a graduate of the China Conservatory of Music. During my undergraduate studies at BUPT, I joined AiYue Chorus as a conductor, guided by Mrs. Ma Menghe (马梦鹤), Mr. Li Hong (李闳), and Mr. Li Houpu (李厚朴).

In recent years, my musical interests have moved toward R&B, Soul, and Jazz. I joined the NUS Jazz Band as a vocalist and also perform at the Sound and Music Computing Lab's annual concerts. Music remains my way of shaping emotion, attention, and communication.
